DUSK

A lofi beat maker for the M-VAVE FM-1.

⚠ Not yet tried on a real FM-1. Dusk is built and tested on every change, in CI and in the FM-1 emulator, but it has not been tried on an actual device. Install it at your own risk.

The kind of beat that sits on a morning coffee or study playlist: jazzy chords on a soft electric piano, a sub bass under them, lazy drums, a small tune on top, and all of it through a tired tape. You build it from clips: pick one for each of four tracks and it already sounds right; change one and it becomes your own.

KEYS14 progressions, 10 ways to play them, 27 chords on the keys
BASS8 lines that follow the chords' roots
DRUMS10 grooves, 6 kits, swing, a lazy snare
LEAD8 toplines that fit your chords, or play your own

Try it here

The firmware itself, compiled for the browser: the same code as on the FM-1, its sound, its screen, its panel and its keys' lights. Press PLAY, then turn ALGORITHM and PRESETS.

KNOB 1 KNOB 2 KNOB 3 KNOB 4
SELECTthe cursor ALGORITHMthe clip PRESETSthe sound

Start it, then press PLAY. The − and + beside a knob turn it; so does the mouse wheel over it. The keys also play from A W S E D F T G Y H U J K on your keyboard.

Install

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (they can talk to the FM-1 over USB MIDI).
  2. Connect the FM-1 directly to the computer with a USB data cable, and switch it on.
  3. Close other apps that use MIDI, press Install and allow MIDI when the browser asks.

Log

  

  
Do not unplug the cable while it writes (about a minute). If an install is cut off, the FM-1
    stays in update mode: replug it and press Install again, and it finishes. If the FM-1 does not start at all, hold
    OCT− while switching it on: it starts in the USB rescue, where an installer always works. To go back to
    the official firmware, use M-VAVE's own updater M-UPGRADE. Installing firmware is at your own risk.


  
Using it

  

  

    
SELECTThe cursor: the lane, the chord, the step, the place in the song, the tape on the shelf.

    
ALGORITHMThe clip of this track, from the bank. 0 is MINE: your own.

    
PRESETSThe sound of this track.

    
KNOB 1–4What the bottom of the screen says.

    
HOMEThe four lanes. Keys F G A B: the scenes A B C D (hold one, press another: a copy). C D E F: the tracks on / off.

    
ARP · LFO · SEQ · EDITKEYS (every key is a chord), BASS and DRUMS (the white keys are the 16 steps), LEAD (play it; REC writes it).

    
FXThe tape and the room: wobble, vinyl, tone, drive; again: space, pump, crush, rain.

    
GLOThe song: scenes in order. SEL turns it on.

    
SAVEThe tapes: eight of your own, eight from the factory. SEL loads; SAVE again keeps yours.

    
PLAY · REC · ENVStart / stop; write what the keys play; tap the tempo.
